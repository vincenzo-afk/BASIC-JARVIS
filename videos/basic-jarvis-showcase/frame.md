# BASIC-JARVIS motion frame

Use a dark, renderer-faithful interface language: near-black background layers, slightly lifted graphite panels, cyan signal lines, green connected states, and soft glass borders. Use asymmetric layouts with a clear visual lead rather than identical cards. Every scene carries a restrained grid, an ambient orb, or a signal trace so the canvas remains alive while content enters.

Motion uses short, overlapping GSAP entrances with `power3.out`, staggered opacity/translation, and one slow ambient transform per scene. Keep all text large enough to read at 1920×1080 and avoid pure white or pure black where tinted neutrals are available.
