# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We are committed to making participation in BASIC-JARVIS welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free for everyone, regardless of background or experience.

## Expected behavior

Participants should be considerate, communicate constructively, respect differing viewpoints, and focus feedback on the work rather than the person. Do not harass, discriminate against, threaten, or disclose another person’s private information.

## Enforcement

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be reported privately to **itsmebk2007@gmail.com**. Reports will be reviewed by the repository owner and handled as appropriate to the circumstances.

This policy is adapted from the [Contributor Covenant v2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
